# ASHVALE facts for @ashvale (update after every release; public, so no other game names)

## What it is
- ASHVALE is a 3D fantasy adventure game inscribed on the chain at DogecoinArcade.com. Open it from the Games tab.
  It plays in any browser, desktop or phone.
- It is made by @ashvale. Only items inscribed by @ashvale and tokens issued by @ashvale count in the game.
- Play solo, or online with others: players in the same area share the same world, enemies and dropped items.

## Your progress carries over
- The game opens through one launcher inscription that never changes. Each release only inscribes the pieces that
  changed (small modules) plus a new list of them, and the launcher always loads the newest list.
- Your progress is kept as you play and carries into every future release automatically - nothing to do on your side.
  It is kept for the window you play in: finding that same character on a second device does not work yet, and we are
  working on it. A window opened from a link in a feed post keeps nothing at all, and says so on its first card.
- Saves from the old one-page playtest builds (before the launcher) do not carry over.
- Fixes are small: one or two module inscriptions, not a whole new game.

## In the current release (v0.8.4)
- Skills: Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Ranged, Magic, Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Cooking, Firemaking,
  Dexterity, Speechcraft. Spend starting points on attributes.
- The valley: the village (shops, smith, lake) and Whisperwood, with a mine. Trees from plain to yew, ores from copper
  to mithril, fishing from shrimp (net) to lobster (lobster pot).
- Weight matters: packs carry more; overburdened players slow down.
- Inventory drag and drop: drag between slots, onto the Equipment tab to wear, or off the panel to drop on the ground.
  On a phone, hold briefly then drag.
- Firemaking: buy a tinderbox from Tam, tap logs to light a campfire, cook on it. Better logs need a higher level.
- Frost staff (Garrick's, 600 GOLD, Magic 20): its spells can freeze an enemy for a few seconds. More abilities such as
  poison can be added later as item data.
- Weather: fog, rain and snow. Fog shortens sight and bow/spell range (for enemies too), rain makes fires fail more and
  burn out sooner, snow slows running a little. Everyone in an area sees the same weather.
- Enemies fight back: if you attack one from outside its area, it comes after you or shoots back.
- Death drops everything you carry and wear as one pile; anyone can pick it up.
- Animals drop pelts (sell them in town); armed enemies drop what they carry.
- Trades: NFT for NFT, or NFT for GOLD (token #26).
- Male or female characters. Tap or click to walk, double-tap to run.
- Settings has "Run: always", so you don't have to double-tap every move. Hold Shift while you click for the opposite
  of that click: walk with running switched on, run with it off. On a phone, "Run: always" is the whole control.
- Open ASHVALE from a link in a feed post and the first card says plainly that this window cannot keep your progress;
  open it from the Games tab and press Play there to keep your character.
- The world (v0.8.2): a small planet, mostly ocean, with two big continents, island chains and lakes. Rivers run from
  mountain creeks to the sea and widen as they join; frozen lakes in the cold can be walked on.
- Climates: desert, steppe, savanna, grassland, temperate forest, rainforest, taiga and tundra, each with its own trees
  and ground. Mountain ranges have passes and valleys, a tree line, and snow only on the high tops.
- Weather follows the climate: the desert is nearly always clear, the rainforest wet, the taiga snowy and foggy.
- Saltmere: a harbour town on the coast, a short trail from Ashvale (about 500 m), with piers, warehouses, a market,
  a harbour master and houses two and three storeys tall. Walk into a tall house and click the stairs to climb; floors
  above you go see-through. Monsters do not follow you upstairs.
- Water reads as one flat surface, at home or at sea: no shelves or steps where it meets the shore, and the piers and
  the quay at Saltmere sit at the waterline. The fishing pond by Ashvale village is a shallow pond you can stand in.
- Animals (v0.8.4): chickens and hens wander the towns and scatter when you run past. Out in the wild the herds fit the
  land - deer in the woods, hares in the open country, goats on the heights, sand lizards in the desert, snow hares in
  the far north, wild boars on the beaches by Saltmere. Timber wolves hunt in the deep wild and will follow anyone who
  strays too far from town; they keep away from the roads and the fields. Hunting an animal gives the same kind of reward
  as fighting: you get Attack, Strength and Defence experience for it.
- A Wallet tab under the settings gear shows the ASHVALE gear and tokens your arcade wallet holds. It only reads: it
  never moves anything, and it cannot spend or send anything.
- If you click a monster you cannot walk to, the game now says "You can't reach that from here." instead of saying
  nothing.
- Quest: The Ashen Crown.
- v0.5: fog is measured around you and the camera, so your character never disappears in thick fog; New character in
  Settings really starts over; the shop screen keeps dimmed items in place; monsters no longer stand frozen when you
  come back to an area someone else stayed in.
