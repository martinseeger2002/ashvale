WIND
Swing: press SWING (or Space) to start, press again to set the power (the gold MAX line is full power; right on it gives a bonus, past it you lose a little), the marker runs to the left and comes back - press a third time when it is on the white line at the right. Early or late curves the ball.
Aim: drag the course, or the arrow buttons / ← →.
Clubs: ◀ ▶ or ↑ ↓. Spin: 1 top, 2 none, 3 back. Putts: Short (up to 72 ft) for a fine touch near the hole, Long (up to 264 ft) from far away; keys 1 / 2.
On the green the dots drift downhill: read the break. The red flag on the meter marks the power for the hole's distance (on the flat; uphill needs more, downhill less).
