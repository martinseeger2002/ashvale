The Saltmere Links, better: a group now takes turns like real golf. Tee off in order, then the ball farthest from the hole plays. Everyone watches the player's shot, and the others stand round to watch until it is their turn.

The swing works on phones: one touch is one press, so you always get the strike tap after the power tap. The course is smooth mown turf, the ponds sit in the ground, and a cobbled road with lamps runs from Saltmere's streets to the clubhouse.
